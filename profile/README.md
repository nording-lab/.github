# Nording Lab — Chronic Inflammation in Vascular Diseases

Research group of **Prof. Dr. med. Henry Nording**, W1 tenure-track professor for Chronic Inflammation in Vascular Diseases at the **Department of Internal Medicine V – Angiology**, University Medical Center Schleswig-Holstein (UKSH), Campus Kiel, and the Medical Faculty of Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel. Full member of the *Precision Medicine in Chronic Inflammation (PMI)* Excellence Cluster.

## Research focus

Our research focuses on cardiovascular disease, the immune system in cardiovascular disease, and vascular adaptation to atherosclerosis — i.e. collateral artery formation. In basic science, our focus is **platelet biology**, which we regard as part of cardiovascular immune interactions, as we see platelets as immune cells.

We also develop AI-supported diagnostic tools and run the **CytoIntelligence Core Facility** for AI-based flow-cytometry data analysis.

## This organization

Reproducible pipelines and tools for our flow-cytometry and cardiovascular research. Repositories are private during development and released openly at publication.
