# Nording Lab — Chronic Inflammation in Vascular Diseases

Research group of **Prof. Dr. med. Henry Nording**, W1 tenure-track professor of Chronic Inflammation in Vascular Diseases at the **Department of Internal Medicine V – Angiology**, UKSH Campus Kiel and Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel, and member of the [Precision Medicine in Chronic Inflammation (PMI)](https://www.precisionmedicine.de/de/personen/nording-henry-70125) Excellence Cluster.

## Research focus

We study cardiovascular disease and the immune system's role in it, and how the vasculature adapts to atherosclerosis through collateral artery formation. At the bench our focus is **platelet biology** — which we regard as cardiovascular immunology, treating platelets as immune cells. We also develop AI-supported diagnostics and run the **CytoIntelligence Core Facility** for AI-based flow-cytometry analysis.

## Repositories

Reproducible pipelines and tools for our flow-cytometry and cardiovascular research — private during development, released openly at publication.
